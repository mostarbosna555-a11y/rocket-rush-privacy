🚀 Rocket Rush — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026 · App: Rocket Rush (com.rocketrush.game)

This Privacy Policy describes how Rocket Rush ("the Game", "we") handles information when you play the Game on Android. We designed the Game to collect as little data as possible: we do not ask for your real name, e-mail address, phone number, precise location, or contacts, and the Game has no account registration.

1. Information stored on your device only

Game progress (coins, high scores, unlocked rockets, missions, achievements, settings, and the pilot nickname and country flag you choose) is stored locally on your device. It never leaves your device except as described in Section 2, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game or use the in-game "Reset progress" option.

2. Online leaderboard (Google Firebase)

If your device is online, the Game submits your best score to a global leaderboard using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). What is stored on the leaderboard:

  • a randomly generated anonymous player ID (created by Firebase Anonymous Authentication);
  • the pilot nickname you typed (please do not use your real name);
  • the country flag you selected (chosen by you — we do not use GPS or location services);
  • your best score and the time it was achieved.

Nicknames and scores on the leaderboard are visible to other players. Firebase is operated by Google LLC; see the Firebase Privacy information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows advertisements served by Google AdMob (interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads). AdMob may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, and coarse (non-precise) location derived from IP, to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Permissions used

  • Internet / network state — for ads and the online leaderboard;
  • Vibration — haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings);
  • Notifications — local reminders scheduled on your device (e.g. daily reward); these are not push notifications from a server and can be disabled in system settings.

5. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no chat, no social features beyond the nickname leaderboard, and no real-money purchases. If you are a parent and believe your child submitted a nickname that identifies them, contact us and we will remove it.

6. Data retention & deletion

Local data: delete via uninstall or "Reset progress". Leaderboard data: e-mail us your in-game nickname and we will delete the record. Anonymous authentication records are retained by Firebase until deleted.

7. Security

Leaderboard writes are restricted by Firebase security rules so that each anonymous account can only modify its own entry. No payment or sensitive personal data is processed by the Game.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the Game evolves (for example, if online multiplayer rooms are added). Updates will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
